Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026. This policy covers 50bridgesinsuranceservices.com, operated by Fifty Bridges Insurance Services, Inc.

What this site does

This site provides a lookup tool that displays public records about motor carriers from the Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration (FMCSA). The information shown — carrier names, addresses, authority status, and insurance filing history — is public government data about businesses, not private personal data.

What we collect

Lookup queries. When you enter a US DOT number, our server requests that carrier's public record from FMCSA systems and displays it to you. We do not build profiles of visitors from lookups, and we do not sell lookup data.

Contact by email or phone. If you email or call us, we receive the information you send (such as your name, email address, and message) and use it only to respond and to provide the insurance services you ask about.

Quote requests. Quote requests are handled on our main website, 50bridges.com, under its own process; this site does not operate its own quote form.

Cookies and tracking. This site sets no advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability.

What we share

We do not sell personal information. We share information only as needed to provide services you request (for example, with insurance carriers or underwriters when you ask us for a quote through our main website) or when required by law.

Third-party sources

Carrier data comes from FMCSA systems (safer.fmcsa.dot.gov and FMCSA open data). Their handling of that public data is governed by the U.S. Department of Transportation's own policies.
